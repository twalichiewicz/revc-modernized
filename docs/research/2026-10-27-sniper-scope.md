# Sniper scope: Vice City's today, picture-in-picture, or a modern full-screen scope

The player asked for the sniper rifle's scope to be redone, "either as PiP or other modern ways of handling it". This note covers how the game's scope works and how the mod enters it. It then looks at whether a picture-in-picture (PiP) lens is feasible in this engine, describes how console shooters handle a full-screen scope, and recommends one approach with its hook points.

**Recommendation.** Keep the game's own sniper camera (`MODE_SNIPER`) and replace how the scope is shown: the gun is raised to the eye, then the view goes to a full-screen scope with sway, held breath, zoom stops, no green ghosting, and the game's own scope art redrawn with a black surround. This approach costs nothing in frame time and keeps the game's LOD, firing and laser dot. It needs at most one new binary hook. PiP is possible, but the world would be drawn twice, and distant targets would drop to LODs or fade out inside the lens. Details are in §4.

## 1. How the game does the scope today

### 1.1 Camera modes

These come from the `CCam` mode enum, [Camera.h L37–84](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Camera.h#L37-L84). `DefaultFOV` is 70 ([L30](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Camera.h#L30)). The ids match the mod's `CamModeId` in `src/game.h:421`. **Verified.**

| Id | Mode | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | `MODE_FOLLOWPED` | The on-foot camera. The mod's first person rides on it. |
| 7 | `MODE_SNIPER` | Both sniper rifles: `CCam::Process_Sniper` |
| 8 | `MODE_ROCKETLAUNCHER` | `Process_Rocket`, with a fixed FOV of 70 ([Cam.cpp L2219](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Cam.cpp#L2219)) |
| 34 | `MODE_M16_1STPERSON` | M4, Ruger, M60. The mod skips it with `skip:rifle_first_person`. |
| 39–42 | `*_RUNABOUT` | The PC's walk-around variants. `ProcessPlayerWeapon` never sets these. |
| 45 / 46 | `MODE_HELICANNON_1STPERSON` / `MODE_CAMERA` | `MODE_CAMERA` also runs `Process_Sniper`, with an FOV floor of 3° |

`CCam::Process` sends both 7 and 46 to `Process_Sniper` ([Cam.cpp L197–199](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Cam.cpp#L197-L199)).

### 1.2 Entering, firing, leaving (**Verified** in source)

- **Entering.** `CPlayerPed::ProcessPlayerWeapon` checks for `TargetJustDown()` while holding weapon type 28 (sniper), 29 (laser scope) or 30 (rocket launcher). It then calls `TheCamera.SetNewPlayerWeaponMode(MODE_SNIPER or MODE_ROCKETLAUNCHER)`, zeroes his speed, blends the idle animation and sets `PED_SNIPER_MODE` ([PlayerPed.cpp L1267–1293](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp#L1267-L1293)). Outside a first-person mode these three weapons return before the fire code, so there is no hip fire ([L1295–1297](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp#L1295-L1297)). A trigger pull plays a "no zoom" click instead ([L1786–1794](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp#L1786-L1794)).
- **While scoped.** `PED_SNIPER_MODE` runs `PlayerControlSniper` ([L1854–1858](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp#L1854-L1858)). He cannot walk, but he can duck ([L682–688](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp#L682-L688)). Letting go of the target button calls `RestorePreviousState` and `ClearPlayerWeaponMode` ([L690–694](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp#L690-L694)). A shot needs `WeaponJustDown` and the weapon's own timer to have run out, then goes through `CWeapon::Fire` ([L696–701](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/peds/PlayerPed.cpp#L696-L701)). The 266/333 ms `firingRate` there only times the echo sound.
- **The shot.** `CWeapon::FireSniper` refuses the player's shot unless the active camera is in a scope mode (7, 8, 34, 46 or a runabout). The bullet leaves from `cam->Source` along `cam->Front` (`CBulletInfo::AddBullet`), with a pad shake and `CamShakeNoPos(0.2)` ([Weapon.cpp L2269–2327](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/weapons/Weapon.cpp#L2269-L2327)). Rockets have the same camera-mode check in `FireProjectile` ([L2091–2101](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/weapons/Weapon.cpp#L2091-L2101)). Whatever the camera's centre is aimed at is where the bullet goes.

### 1.3 `CCam::Process_Sniper` ([Cam.cpp L2870–3038](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Cam.cpp#L2870-L3038), **Verified**)

- **Eye.** The eye is at his head bone + 0.1 m up, pulled 0.19 m back along his facing (ducking uses `fDuckingBackOffset`/`fDuckingRightOffset` instead) (L2896–2912). The mod's first-person eye is 0.08 m up and 0.06 m *ahead* of the head (`src/camera.cpp:57`), so cutting between the two moves the eye by about a quarter of a metre (**Inferred**).
- **Look.** Mouse input wins: `Beta += m_fMouseAccelHorzntl × (−3·MouseX) × FOV/80`, and the same for pitch with 4·MouseY. Otherwise the **left** stick drives the look through `CPad::SniperModeLookLeftRight` and `SniperModeLookUpDown`. Those have a 35/128 dead zone, rescaled ([Pad.cpp L3303–3341](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Pad.cpp#L3303-L3341)). The stick is squared: `(x/100)²·0.8/14·FOV/80` per 1/50 s step (L2916–2941). **Derived:** full stick turns about 235°/s at FOV 70 and about 50°/s at FOV 15. Pitch is clamped to +60°/−89.5° (L2944–2945).
- **Zoom.** `SniperZoomIn` and `SniperZoomOut` are Square and Cross in controller configs 0, 1 and 3 ([Pad.cpp L3247–3299](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Pad.cpp#L3247-L3299)); that is X and A on an Xbox pad. Holding one multiplies or divides FOV by `(255·step + 10000)/10000` each step. When the mouse wheel is bound, each notch moves ±10° and is eased by `WellBufferMe` (L2951–2985). FOV is clamped to 15–70° (L2989–2997). **Derived:** about 3.5× per second, so 70°→15° takes about 1.2 s. Magnification relative to a 70° view is tan 35°/tan 7.5° ≈ **5.3×**.
- **Every frame.** It sets `SetMotionBlur(180,255,180,120, MOTION_BLUR_SNIPER)` (L2987). It pulls the near plane to 0.4 m when line-of-sight tests around the eye fail (L3003–3031). It turns the ped to the view (L3035–3037).

### 1.4 The overlay (**Verified** in source; the textures were read from the game's `hud.txd`)

- **The scope sprite.** `CHud::Draw` sets `DrawCrossHair` for modes 7, 8, 34, 45 and 46 ([Hud.cpp L242–244](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Hud.cpp#L242-L244)). For the rifles it draws one quarter-sprite four times, mirrored, into a 420×420-unit square around the screen centre ([L326–368](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Hud.cpp#L326-L368)). That sprite is `HUD_SITESNIPER` (`sitesniper`/`sitesniperm`), or `HUD_SITELASER` for the laser rifle ([L205–208](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Hud.cpp#L205-L208)). Nothing is drawn outside that square (**Inferred:** on 16:9 the world shows at the sides).
- **The textures.** In `GameData/models/hud.txd`, `sitesniper` and `sitelaser` are 512×512 DXT3 quarters, so the scope is 1024 px across. Each has a hard-edged black corner. `sitesniper` has a thin green duplex reticle: two ticks per arm, a ring at about 1/3 radius, and a centre circle with a red dot. `sitelaser` has red ticks only. `siterocket` is also 512²; `siteM16` is 128².
- **The laser dot.** For the laser rifle, `CWeapon::LaserScopeDot` traces from the camera along `Front` for the weapon's range. It projects the hit with `CSprite::CalcScreenCoors` and registers a red corona ([Weapon.cpp L2233–2263](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/weapons/Weapon.cpp#L2233-L2263)). `CHud` draws `laserdot` there as a flickering XLU sprite ([Hud.cpp L369–392](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Hud.cpp#L369-L392)). The rocket launcher draws `siterocket` additively at the centre (L316–326).
- **Green ghosting.** `MOTION_BLUR_SNIPER` makes `CPostFX` always keep the last frame. It blends that frame, tinted (180,255,180), at alpha 80/255 over the new one ([postfx.cpp L249–262, L360–363, L417–419](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/extras/postfx.cpp#L249-L262)). That is the scope's green tint, and it smears while you turn.

### 1.5 How the mod uses it today (**Verified** in the repo)

- **Entering.** LT holds the game's target button (pad RB, `src/controls.cpp:263-275`), so the game enters `MODE_SNIPER` itself. `fpshoot` never fires types 28, 29 or 30 (`src/fpshoot.cpp:18,40`). The spec says so too: scoped weapons "fire only from the game's own scope view" (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-first-person-gunplay-design.md:196-198`).
- **The mod's first person steps aside.** `camera::OurCamera` needs mode 4 or 36 (`src/camera.cpp:227-233`), so in mode 7 neither the mod's first person nor its viewmodel runs. One note: `camera.cpp:33` describes `m_bFirstPersonBeingUsed` as the scopes' flag. In reVC it is the classic look-around first person ([Camera.cpp L1018–1046](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Camera.cpp#L1018-L1046)); it is the mode test that keeps the mod out of the scope.
- **Look.** `StickLook` zeroes both sticks in a scope. It feeds the right stick into the camera's mouse input, at the mod's rates times `fov/70` (`src/controls.cpp:513-538`). Process_Sniper's own left-stick path never runs.

## 2. PiP: a second render of the world into the lens

### 2.1 What librw offers (**Verified** in source; in the binary as noted)

- **Offscreen cameras.** librw's gl3 backend gives a `CAMERATEXTURE` raster a texture plus its own FBO ([gl3raster.cpp L138–206](https://github.com/aap/librw/blob/18532c2e13efbc1aa43f0b00b4459831f9414de0/src/gl/gl3raster.cpp#L138-L206)). A `ZBUFFER` raster is a depth-stencil texture, or a renderbuffer on GLES ([L233–268](https://github.com/aap/librw/blob/18532c2e13efbc1aa43f0b00b4459831f9414de0/src/gl/gl3raster.cpp#L233-L268)); macVC runs GLES through ANGLE. `beginUpdate` builds the view and projection from the camera's frame and view window, attaches the z raster to the FBO and sets the viewport ([gl3device.cpp L1196–1240, L1290–1367](https://github.com/aap/librw/blob/18532c2e13efbc1aa43f0b00b4459831f9414de0/src/gl/gl3device.cpp#L1290-L1367)). `endUpdate` is empty (L1369–1372), so switching cameras is just another `BeginUpdate`.
- **The calls exist in macVC.** These are all in the binary: `_Z14RwCameraCreatev`, `_Z14RwRasterCreateiiii`, `_Z17RwCameraSetRasterPN2rw6CameraEPNS_6RasterE`, `_Z18RwCameraSetZRasterPN2rw6CameraEPNS_6RasterE`, `_Z19RwCameraBeginUpdatePN2rw6CameraE`, `_Z17RwCameraEndUpdatePN2rw6CameraE`, `_Z13RwCameraClearPN2rw6CameraEPNS_4RGBAEi`, `_Z21RwCameraSetViewWindowPN2rw6CameraEPKNS_3V2dE`, `_Z15RwTextureCreatePN2rw6RasterE`, plus `glad_glGenFramebuffers` and `glad_glFramebufferTexture2D`. librw's `rasterCreateCameraTexture` is static and not exported. Indirect evidence that camera textures work in this build: macVC exports `rw::gl3::rasterCreate` and the local symbols `beginUpdate` and `setFrameBuffer`. `CPostFX::Open` also creates two camera-texture rasters ([postfx.cpp L58–59](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/extras/postfx.cpp#L58-L59)), and the mod already reads one of them every frame (`src/shaders.cpp:133-134`).
- **A pattern to follow.** `CShadowCamera::Create` is in macVC (`_ZN13CShadowCamera6CreateEi`). It builds exactly the camera a PiP pass needs ([ShadowCamera.cpp L72–114](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/ShadowCamera.cpp#L72-L114)). Cutscene shadows draw into it before the main camera begins: "has to be called before BeginUpdate because of cutscene shadows" ([main.cpp L1247](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/main.cpp#L1247)).

### 2.2 What would be drawn again

`Idle` builds the render list (L1591), runs `CRenderer::PreRender` (L1595), begins the main camera through `DoRWStuffStartOfFrame_Horizon` (L1606–1611), then calls `RenderScene` (L1623), `RenderEffects` (L1631), `RenderMotionBlur` (L1644) and `Render2dStuff` (L1648) ([main.cpp](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/main.cpp#L1591-L1648)).

`RenderScene` is ([L1350–1375](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/main.cpp#L1350-L1375)): `CClouds::Render`, `DoRWRenderHorizon`, `CRenderer::RenderRoads`, `CCoronas::RenderReflections`, `RenderEverythingBarRoads`, `CWaterLevel::RenderWater`, `RenderBoats`, `RenderFadingInUnderwaterEntities`, `RenderTransparentWater`, `RenderFadingInEntities`, `CWeather::RenderRainStreaks` and `CCoronas::RenderSunReflection`. `RenderFirstPersonVehicle`, the mod's viewmodel hook, comes last in `RenderEffects` (L1417).

All of these are in macVC with the reVC signatures. `_Z11RenderScenev`, `_Z17DoRWRenderHorizonv`, `_ZN9CRenderer11RenderRoadsEv`, `_ZN9CRenderer24RenderEverythingBarRoadsEv`, `_ZN9CRenderer22RenderFadingInEntitiesEv`, `_ZN11CWaterLevel11RenderWaterEv`, `_ZN7CClouds6RenderEv` and `_ZN9CRenderer19ConstructRenderListEv` were checked by name.

macVC also contains the `NEW_RENDERER` path (`RenderScene_new`, `gbNewRenderer`). This player's `macVC.ini` has `NewRenderer=0`, so the path above is the one in use.

### 2.3 Render lists, frustum and LOD

- **Direction.** `ConstructRenderList` → `ScanWorld` builds one list per frame from the main camera's frustum ([Renderer.cpp L956–981](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Renderer.cpp#L956-L981)). A narrower view along the same axis from the same eye is a subset of that frustum, so nothing is missing (**Inferred**).
- **LOD is chosen by distance alone.** The distance is measured from the camera and scaled by `TheCamera.LODDistMultiplier`: `GetAtomicFromDistance` returns the first atomic whose `m_lodDistances[i] × multiplier` exceeds the distance ([SimpleModelInfo.cpp L139–149](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/modelinfo/SimpleModelInfo.cpp#L139-L149)). `SetupEntityVisibility` sets that geometry on the entity for the frame ([Renderer.cpp L745–772](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Renderer.cpp#L745-L772)). Big-building LODs are shown beyond each model's near distance ([L874](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Renderer.cpp#L874)). Peds drop to LOD at `60 × multiplier` m and fade by `70 × multiplier` m ([VisibilityPlugins.cpp L160–161](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/rw/VisibilityPlugins.cpp#L144-L161)).
- **The multiplier follows the field of view.** `CCamera::Process` sets it to `min(70/FOV, 2.2) × CRenderer::ms_lodDistScale` ([Camera.cpp L617–632](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/core/Camera.cpp#L617-L632)). The game's own scope therefore draws further detail on its own; at FOV ≤ 31.8° it reaches the 2.2 cap. With PiP the main view stays wide, so the multiplier stays at 1.0. **Derived** from this player's `DrawDistance=1.8` (`ms_lodDistScale`): peds fade at **126 m in a PiP lens versus 277 m in the game's scope**, and every building swaps to its LOD 2.2× closer. Insurgency: Sandstorm shipped a fix for exactly this: "distant LOD in scopes not loading while using PIP scope settings" (Update 1.16 notes, **secondary**: [MP1st's copy](https://mp1st.com/news/insurgency-sandstorm-update-1-16-brings-loads-of-fixes)).
- **Fix and its cost.** Raise `LODDistMultiplier` (`TheCamera+0xf8`; the mod already scales it in `src/traffic.cpp:17,216`) to the zoom's value while scoped. That also loads and draws more detail in the wide main view.

### 2.4 What assumes one camera

- **Game.** `RenderEverythingBarRoads` pushes alpha entities into `CVisibilityPlugins::m_alphaEntityList` ([Renderer.cpp L312](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Renderer.cpp#L312)). That list is cleared only in `ScanWorld` ([L1034](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Renderer.cpp#L1034)), so a second call doubles the fading entities. A PiP pass needs its own loop over `CRenderer::ms_aVisibleEntityPtrs` with `RenderOneRoad`/`RenderOneNonRoad`, or must save and restore the list; all four symbols are in macVC. `CVisibilityPlugins::SetRenderWareCamera` (in macVC) must be pointed at the PiP camera and back. Coronas, particles, the sun, the clouds and the laser dot project with `TheCamera.m_viewMatrix` and `CDraw`'s FOV in `CSprite::CalcScreenCoors` ([Sprite.cpp L27–48](https://github.com/mrxenginner/reVC/blob/d7dd1354d258762480ab7dec56fa0553fa0e951a/src/renderer/Sprite.cpp#L27-L48)). In the lens they would be misplaced unless skipped or the camera fields were swapped (**Inferred**).
- **The mod** (I grepped `src/` for `RenderScene`, `RwCameraBeginUpdate`, sky, clouds, `rtshadows` and shaders):
  - `sky.cpp` hooks `CClouds::Render`. Its volumetric clouds reproject a history buffer sized to the frame and keyed to the camera, and a size change or a cut resets it (`src/sky.cpp:109-132`). A lens pass through that hook would wipe the history every frame, so it must call the original or skip it.
  - `grade.cpp` hooks `RenderMotionBlur` and `shaders::GrabFrame` reads `CPostFX`'s screen-sized back buffer (`src/grade.cpp:182,234`; `src/shaders.h:64-67`). These are fine as long as the PiP pass never goes through them.
  - `fpgun.cpp` clears depth and draws in the main camera's projection (`src/fpgun.cpp:372,416-422`).
  - `camera.cpp` hooks `SetupEntityVisibility` (`src/camera.cpp:486`), which is harmless for a second pass.
  - Nothing in `src/` calls `RwCameraBeginUpdate` or hooks `RenderScene` today.
  - The port's Metal ray-traced shadows (`CRTShadows::Render`, in macVC; `src/rtshadows.h:2-6`) are an unknown. I didn't find where they composite, so a lens pass may come out without them.
- **Firing.** With PiP the active camera is mode 4, so `FireSniper` rejects the player's shot (§1.2). The mod would have to switch the camera's mode to 7 around the `Fire` call, the way `Hook_SetupEntityVisibility` switches it (`src/camera.cpp:486-495`), or add bullets itself (`_ZN11CBulletInfo9AddBulletEP7CEntity11eWeaponType7CVectorS3_` is in macVC).

### 2.5 Cost

Not measured. A PiP pass sends every visible world entity to the GPU a second time, roughly doubling the world pass's CPU cost: VC draws the world as many small draw calls, and this port runs them through ANGLE→Metal (**Inferred**). The GPU cost grows with the lens target: 1024² is 28% of a 2560×1440 frame. Before committing to PiP, time `_Z11RenderScenev` on this machine; that is the price of the second pass. It also needs new binary hooks. `CRenderer::PreRender` or `RenderScene` are not in `DEFAULT_HOOKS` (`tools/patch_macvc.py:58`), and the stub padding is a limited resource (the docstring, L1–25).

## 3. The full-screen scope as console shooters do it

I found no developer talk or official document from Infinity Ward or DICE describing how their scopes are rendered or tuned. Except for the CoD4 code below, everything here is **convention**.

- **Raise, then cut to an overlay that fades in.** In CoD4 the scope overlay's alpha is `zoomFrac`, which runs from 0 to 1 over the last `fAdsZoomInFrac` of the aim-down-sights blend ([cg_draw_reticles.cpp L48–80, L450–475](https://github.com/SwagSoftware/KisakCOD/blob/8aadf94cc82795d6829ca06052996a5ec79001c3/src/cgame/cg_draw_reticles.cpp#L450-L475)).
- **The overlay follows the gun, not the screen centre.** CoD4 draws it at `CG_CalcCrosshairPosition`, the gun's pitch and yaw projected into the view (L17–46), so the black ring and the "scope shadow" move with sway. It uses the same four mirrored quarters as VC. `CG_DrawFrameOverlay` then fills the rest of the screen with the material's black edge (L204–380).
- **Sway and held breath.** CoD4's idle sway is two sines on yaw and pitch. Its amplitude is the weapon's idle amount × a crouch/prone factor × the ADS fraction × `holdBreathScale` ([bg_weapons.cpp L4211–4285](https://github.com/SwagSoftware/KisakCOD/blob/8aadf94cc82795d6829ca06052996a5ec79001c3/src/bgame/bg_weapons.cpp#L4211-L4285)). Breath can be held only when fully aimed with a scoped weapon; the sway then eases to zero. After `player_breath_hold_time` **4.5 s** it forces a gasp of `player_breath_gasp_time` **1.0 s**, with sway up to `player_breath_gasp_scale` **4.5×** that decays ([bg_weapons.cpp L1502–1567](https://github.com/SwagSoftware/KisakCOD/blob/8aadf94cc82795d6829ca06052996a5ec79001c3/src/bgame/bg_weapons.cpp#L1502-L1567); [bg_misc.cpp L731–770](https://github.com/SwagSoftware/KisakCOD/blob/8aadf94cc82795d6829ca06052996a5ec79001c3/src/bgame/bg_misc.cpp#L731-L770)). Sprint and breath share one command, `+breath_sprint` ([cl_input.cpp L1010–1011](https://github.com/SwagSoftware/KisakCOD/blob/8aadf94cc82795d6829ca06052996a5ec79001c3/src/client_mp/cl_input.cpp#L1010-L1011)); on pads it sits on L3 (**convention**).
- **Counter-Strike 1.6, the feel bar.**
  - The AWP has two zoom stops, 40° and 10° from 90° (2.7× and 11.4×). Firing drops the zoom, and it comes back once the bolt has cycled ([wpn_awp.cpp L75–92, L119–137](https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/blob/4a50c42e85fd3778c2b7d24731c7d30c83bbab01/regamedll/dlls/wpn_shared/wpn_awp.cpp#L75-L137); [weapons.cpp L999–1013](https://github.com/rehlds/ReGameDLL_CS/blob/4a50c42e85fd3778c2b7d24731c7d30c83bbab01/regamedll/dlls/weapons.cpp#L999-L1013)).
  - The viewmodel is hidden at FOV ≤ 40 ([view.cpp L903–905](https://github.com/Velaron/cs16-client/blob/a238ef131803ed7592c4ba701499027c862889ee/cl_dll/view.cpp#L903-L905)).
  - The scope is four arc textures, solid black bars to the screen edges, and one-pixel crosshair lines drawn at screen resolution ([sniperscope.cpp L82–109](https://github.com/Velaron/cs16-client/blob/a238ef131803ed7592c4ba701499027c862889ee/cl_dll/hud/sniperscope.cpp#L82-L109)).
  - Look sensitivity scales with `fov/default × 1.2` ([hud.cpp L345](https://github.com/Velaron/cs16-client/blob/a238ef131803ed7592c4ba701499027c862889ee/cl_dll/hud.cpp#L345); see `fps-feel-controller.md`).
- **Also conventional, no primary source found:** variable zoom on high-power optics, scope glint visible to enemies, zeroing (Battlefield), and slow movement while scoped. **PiP lenses** appear mostly in sim-leaning shooters: Insurgency: Sandstorm offers it as a setting (§2.3), and Escape from Tarkov's 0.12.5 notes promised better "performance while aiming" after an optics rework (**secondary**: [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/escape-from-tarkov/escape-from-tarkov-devs-reveal-long-awaited-changes-in-12-5-patch-notes-1358201/)).

## 4. Recommendation

| | Full-screen modern scope on `MODE_SNIPER` | PiP lens on the viewmodel |
|---|---|---|
| Frame cost | None extra; the narrower frustum draws less | A second world pass (CPU roughly ×2 on the world), plus the lens fill |
| LOD and targets | Correct on its own: 70/FOV, up to 2.2× (§2.3) | Peds fade at 126 m and buildings LOD early, unless the multiplier is raised, which makes the main view heavier |
| Firing, laser dot, near clip | The game's own code, unchanged | The mode check needs a workaround; the laser dot must be re-projected into the lens |
| Mod systems at risk | None (the overlay goes through the existing HUD hook) | `sky.cpp` history, coronas/particles/clouds, the alpha list, RT shadows |
| New binary hooks | 0–1 (`Process_Sniper`, optional) | 1–2 (`PreRender` or `RenderScene`, plus the mode swap) |
| Fidelity / feel | Vice City's own scope art, made modern; CS 1.6 and CoD conventions | Novel for VC; at 5× through a small eyepiece most of the screen is gun |

I recommend **the full-screen scope**. PiP's one gain is seeing around the lens, and it pays for that in every row above. At these magnifications on a pad, CS and CoD both go full-screen.

### 4.1 Hook points (everything below exists today unless marked new)

1. **Raise, then enter.** In `TriggersAimAndFire` (`src/controls.cpp:263`), hold back the game's target button for types 28 and 29 in first person until the viewmodel's aim blend reaches 1. At that point `vm::GunInView` has the eye at the scope's rear sight (`src/vmview.cpp`; `SIGHT_REAR` is on the scope axis in `tools/guns/sniper.py:106`). Then pass the button through, and `ProcessPlayerWeapon` enters `MODE_SNIPER` on `TargetJustDown`.
2. **Camera.** Add `_ZN4CCam14Process_SniperERK7CVectorfff` to `DEFAULT_HOOKS` (**new**). After the original runs:
   - Set `Source` to the mod's eye (no quarter-metre jump).
   - Add sway and the breath scale to `Front`. Shots follow, because `FireSniper` fires along `cam->Front`.
   - Set `FOV` to the mod's zoom stop, eased. FOV below 15° is allowed, since the clamp runs inside the original.

   `CCamera::Process` then derives the matrix, `CDraw` FOV and LOD from those values as usual. Without the new hook, the existing `CCamera::Process` hook can do the same after the original, but it must re-apply the matrix (as `camera.cpp` does for first person) and the LOD stays a frame behind.
3. **Look.** Keep `StickLook`'s scoped path (`src/controls.cpp:513-538`), tuned to `fplook`'s curve and `fov/70`.
4. **Overlay.** In `hud.cpp`'s `CSprite2d::Draw` hook (`src/hud.cpp:252`), replace the draws of `CHud::Sprites` index 63 (`sitesniper`) and 65 (`sitelaser`). `CHud::Sprites` is in macVC, and each `CSprite2d` is 8 bytes (`src/game.h:412` reads sprite 50 at `+0x190`). Draw the same art centred on the sway point, add black fill to every screen edge and a short feathered edge on the ring, and fade it in over the end of the raise. Leave the laser dot as the game draws it; it is world-projected, so it stays right.
5. **No green smear.** In `grade.cpp`'s `RenderMotionBlur` hook, while scoped, swap `TheCamera.m_BlurType` away from `MOTION_BLUR_SNIPER` around the original. A light green grade can stay for Vice City flavour.
6. **Viewmodel.** `fpgun` draws the raise. Once scoped, mode 7 hides it, as CS 1.6 does.
7. **Firing** stays the game's (`PlayerControlSniper` → `FireSniper`). Optionally, CS's drop-and-resume zoom after each bolt cycle.
8. **Rocket launcher.** It has no zoom (FOV is fixed at 70). It fits better as a normal aim down the iron sights (`tools/guns/rocketla.py:111`), firing through the existing `CWeapon::Fire` hook with the camera mode switched for `FireProjectile`. That is a separate small task.

### 4.2 Decisions for the player

- **The hold-breath button.** L3 is the game's crouch in `PlayerControlSniper`, and R3 is bullet time while aiming (`src/layout.h:20`). CoD uses L3.
- **Zoom.**
  - Stops (CS: 2) or continuous zoom.
  - Which button: the game's are X and A held (§1.3); physical RB is free on foot (`src/layout.h:12-20`).
- **Moving while scoped.** Today he must stand still (`PED_SNIPER_MODE`). CS lets you walk slowly.
