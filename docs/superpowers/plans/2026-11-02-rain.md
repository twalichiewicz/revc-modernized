# GTA IV-Style Rain Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** GTA IV's rain: dense lit streaks, wet glossy ground reflecting the city's lights, puddles and splashes, and drops on the view (the windscreen in cars).

**Architecture:**
- **Baked data:** the baker adds two things to `map.bin` (version 6, after the street names' 5): the outdoor paved ground as a wet-surface mesh, and the places rain can't reach.
- **Pure models:** they decide wetness, puddles, streak placement, drop simulation and drop mode, and are unit-tested.
- **Thin render modules:** they draw through the mod's shader layer:
  - in the world at `C3dMarkers::Render`: wet ground, reflections and puddles;
  - in place of `CWeather::RenderRainStreaks`: the streaks;
  - in the post pass at `CCamera::RenderMotionBlur`: drops on the view.

**Tech Stack:** C++17; GLSL through `shaders.h` (librw gl3 over ANGLE); RwIm3D and RwIm2D; the baker in `tools/bake/`; Makefile tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-rain-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Driven by the game's weather:** the rain amount comes from `CWeather::Rain` (`_ZN8CWeather4RainE`) and wetness from `CWeather::WetRoads` (`_ZN8CWeather8WetRoadsE`). Scripted rain and the hurricane are included.
- **Wind:** the clouds' fixed (7, 3) m/s (`clouds.cpp` `kWindX`, `kWindY`).
- **What gets wet:** all outdoor paved ground (tarmac and pavement surfaces), not just road lanes.
- **No rain where none falls:** not under roofs, awnings or bridges, and not indoors (`CGame::currArea != 0`, plus the game's no-rain cull zones).
- **Drops on the view:**
  - first person on foot, and on bikes and boats: as a lens;
  - a car's seat view: on its windscreen only;
  - aircraft cockpits: as cars;
  - convertibles with the top down: as bikes;
  - **never in third person**.
- **Settings:** each part can be switched off in `vcmodernhud.ini` (`RainStreaks`, `WetGround`, `RainDrops`, `RainSplashes`, all on by default).
- **No game data in the repo.** Meshes come from the player's collision at bake time.
- **Quality bar:** new tests pass under `make test` and the sanitizers, and `make all` has no warnings. There must be no visible frame-rate drop in heavy rain at night downtown on this machine (measured and logged, Task 7).
- **Order:** wet ground, then streaks, drops, splashes and puddles. Each lands working and switchable on its own.

## Review Focus

1. **Rain stopping mid-drive:** the ground dries gradually, puddles shrink, and nothing pops. Pinned in Task 1, `TestDriesSmoothly`.
2. **Going indoors** (an interior, `currArea != 0`) while it rains: no streaks or drops inside. Pinned in Task 4, `TestInteriorNoStreaks`, and Task 5, `TestInteriorNoDrops`.
3. **Downtown at night, with dozens of lights:** reflections are capped by distance and brightness, so the frame rate holds. Pinned in Task 3, `TestReflectionCap`.
4. **Getting into a car with drops on the lens:** they clear, and the windscreen's own drops start. Pinned in Task 5, `TestModeSwitchClears`.
5. **An old `map.bin`** (install not re-run): no wet ground, streaks without cover data apart from interiors, and nothing breaks. Pinned in Task 2, `TestLoadsVersion5`, and Task 3, `TestNoWetMeshDrawsNothing`.

- Test: `tests/test_wetground.cpp` (new)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces, in `Map`:
  - `std::vector<WetCell> wet;`, with `struct WetCell { int16_t cx, cy; std::vector<Vec3> tris; };` covering 64 m cells.
  - `std::vector<Cover> covers;`, with `struct Cover { Vec2 lo, hi; float bottom; };` (a covered box: no rain below `bottom`).
  - `bool CoveredAt(const Map &, Vec3 p);` (true under any cover).
- Baker: `std::vector<WetCell> WetGround(const std::vector<CollisionFace> &, ...)` keeps faces whose `surface` is tarmac or pavement (ids by name from `surface.dat`), that face up (normal z > 0.7), and that aren't under a roof. It welds and simplifies them to at most `kWetTrisPerCell = 3000`.
- Covers come from:
  - building roofs at their absolute height (store each building's street level, currently dropped);
  - awning and bridge-deck collision (currently dropped);
  - `Cullzone.dat` zones with the no-rain flag, as covers reaching the sky.

### Task 3: Wet ground and light reflections

**Files:**
- Create: `src/wetground.h`, `src/wetground.cpp` (drawing), `src/wetmodel.h`, `src/wetmodel.cpp` (pure geometry)
- Modify: the `C3dMarkers::Render` hook path (`checkpoint.cpp`'s, shared), `src/config.h/.cpp` (`WetGround`), the Makefile
- Test: `tests/test_wetmodel.cpp` (new)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's `Weather`; Task 2's `Map::wet`.
- Produces:
  - `std::vector<int> CellsNear(const mapdata::Map &, Vec3 camera, float radius = 150);`
  - `std::vector<Reflection> Reflections(const std::vector<Light> &, Vec3 camera, int cap = 24);`
    - `struct Light { Vec3 pos; Vec3 colour; float radius; };`
    - `struct Reflection { Vec3 quad[4]; Vec3 colour; };`
    - A streak runs on the ground under each light, stretched toward the camera, its length growing with grazing angle.
    - The nearest and brightest are kept up to the cap.
  - `bool wetground::Install(); void wetground::Draw();`
- **The shader:** darkens the albedo by up to 35% with wetness, and adds a Fresnel sky reflection (the sky's colours from `shaders::CurrentSky`) at grazing angles. Depth-tested, with a small z-bias over the ground. Reflections are drawn additively after it.
- **Lights:** from `CPointLights` (as `estatelighting.cpp` reads them) and vehicles' headlights.

### Task 5: Drops on the view (lens and windscreen)

**Files:**
- Create: `src/raindrops.h`, `src/raindrops.cpp` (drawing), `src/dropmodel.h`, `src/dropmodel.cpp` (pure)
- Modify: `src/grade.cpp`'s `CCamera::RenderMotionBlur` path (after the lighting pass), `src/config.h/.cpp` (`RainDrops`)
- Test: `tests/test_dropmodel.cpp` (new)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `enum class DropMode { None, Lens, Windscreen };`
  - `DropMode ModeFor(bool firstPersonOnFoot, bool seatView, int vehicleType, bool topDown, bool interior);`
    - Third person gives `None`, and so does an interior.
    - On foot in first person gives `Lens`.
    - A car, helicopter or plane in the seat view gives `Windscreen`.
    - A bike or boat in the seat view, or a car with its top down, gives `Lens`.
  - `class Drops { public: void Update(float rain, bool exposed, float speed, DropMode, float dt); const std::vector<Drop> &All() const; };`
    - `struct Drop { Vec2 pos; float radius, trail; };`
    - New drops land at a rate set by `rain` while exposed. Touching drops merge; heavy ones trickle down.
    - On the windscreen, speed pushes them up and aside.
    - A change of mode clears them.
- **Drawing:** the frame (`shaders::GrabFrame`) refracted through each drop's lens shape.
  - Windscreen: masked by a target drawn from the car's glass triangles (materials with alpha < 255, as `seateye.cpp` collects them) from the current camera.
